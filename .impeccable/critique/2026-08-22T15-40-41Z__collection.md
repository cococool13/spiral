## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Authored for Spiral in the first two beats; category-interchangeable after that. Corridor + withheld chrome + italic Host Grotesk is a committed idea. After the manifesto the page becomes a `max-w-6xl` featured-row + 3-up card grid that would take another privacy-app maker's names without protest. Other Work is authored, but for Cohen's studio, not for Spiral. Glass is "the one glassmorphism moment" then reused for every CTA.

**Deterministic scan:** CLI exit 2. One `aphoristic-cadence` warning on `collection/out/index.html` (title + hero lines glued by HTML strip). False positive: unique authored rebuttal is the title plus "No account. No bloat. No tracking." Browser inject found `all-caps-body` on Other Work meta (labels), `line-length` ~90 on FAQ `<dd>` (real), `tight-leading` on `.type-heading` 1.12 (token, not body), `low-contrast` 4.1:1 helix red on void for Clean "Deleted for good" (real).

**Visual overlays:** Injection succeeded. Overlays were last left on `/slim/` (7 nodes). Homepage overlays were destroyed by later navigation. No full-page screenshot (page ~5400px; Ego CDP times out). Assessment A had no shell, so its visual pass did not run; synthesis uses Assessment B plus prior Ego DOM facts (Host Grotesk live, footer helix, 380px no overflow).

## Overall Impression

The opening is Spiral. The rest is a catalogue plus a studio index. Biggest opportunity: make the first viewport name the product and the file, then stop restating the thesis at display size.

## What's Working

- Voice states. Hero, manifesto, Wallpaper proof line are the same mind.
- Download honesty is real: one `offerFor`, `noWindowsBinary`, DisabledPill, OS-ready "Download" that does not lie about platform.
- After-hero artifacts cannot be templated: Wallpaper measurements, Slim's 54 policy names, Clean's three verdicts, Resume sheets + fact gate.
