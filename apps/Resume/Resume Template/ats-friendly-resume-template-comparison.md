# ATS-Friendly Resume Templates: Verified Comparison

Research date: August 3, 2026

## Best overall choice

Start with the [Harvard College Bullet Point Resume Template](https://cdn-careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/161/2025/09/2025-template_bullet.docx). It is the most conservative, professional option in this set: a single-column, reverse-chronological layout with familiar headings and bullet points. It is also easy to tailor for most industries.

No template can guarantee that every applicant tracking system (ATS) will parse a resume perfectly. The practical goal is a document with predictable reading order, standard headings, ordinary text, and no essential information in tables, text boxes, graphics, headers, or footers. MIT's career office recommends avoiding text boxes, templates, tables, colors, images, and graphics where ATS compatibility matters; it also recommends conventional fonts and DOC/DOCX or PDF output. Harvard likewise advises a consistent, easily scanned format with standard headings and reverse chronology. [MIT ATS guidance](https://capd.mit.edu/blog/2023/09/01/enhance-your-resume-a-guide-for-first-year-undergraduates/) · [Harvard resume guidance](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/)

## Fast selection guide

| If you are... | Choose | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Applying broadly and want the safest default | Harvard Bullet Point | Clean, restrained, conventional, and easy to tailor. |
| An early-career applicant who needs a short professional summary | University of Washington | Adds a useful summary and flexible sections without a complex layout. |
| A student or recent graduate | MIT Template A | Keeps education and activities prominent in a straightforward chronology. |
| A technical, data, lab, or skills-heavy applicant | MIT Template B | Brings a categorized skills section forward without hiding work history. |
| A mid-career applicant or career changer | Jobscan Hybrid | Combines a summary, skills, accomplishments, and chronological work history. |
| A senior leader with 10+ years of relevant experience | Jobscan Executive | Gives outcomes, leadership scope, and career progression the right emphasis. |
| A mid-career applicant in a traditional field | Jobscan Chronological | Makes the employment timeline the center of the document. |

## Structural ATS check performed

I downloaded and inspected the current `.docx` packages, not just their landing pages. The main document body of every listed file contains zero Word table elements. The Harvard, University of Washington, and MIT files also contain no header or footer parts. The Jobscan files contain a promotional first page and header/footer parts, so clean them before applying and keep all resume content in the document body.

This is a structural screen, not a certification across every vendor's parser. It does not prove that a specific employer's ATS, online form, or PDF conversion will read every version perfectly. Jobscan itself notes that parsing depends on headings, sequencing, tables/images, fonts, and file type. [Jobscan's ATS guidance](https://www.jobscan.co/resume-templates-)

## Detailed comparison

### 1. Harvard College Bullet Point Resume Template

**Download:** [Direct DOCX](https://cdn-careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/161/2025/09/2025-template_bullet.docx) · [official page](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/bullet-point-resume-template/)

- **Layout:** One column; name and contact line; Education; Experience; Leadership & Activities; Skills & Interests. Each role uses action-oriented bullets and a clear reverse-chronological sequence.
- **ATS compatibility:** Strong structural fit. The inspected file has no body tables and no header/footer parts. The headings and reading order are conventional.
- **Ideal use case:** Most students, recent graduates, and professionals who want a conservative, all-purpose template. It works especially well for consulting, finance, operations, government, nonprofit, and general corporate applications.
- **Customization:** Reorder Education and Experience for your seniority; remove high school, coursework, and optional interests when they no longer add value; rename Leadership & Activities to Projects, Selected Projects, or Volunteer Experience when appropriate.
- **File formats:** Direct `.docx`. The official page also offers an accessible Word version and a Google Docs copy option; the Google path may ask you to sign in.
- **Pros:** Professional without looking generic; clear action-bullet guidance; minimal structure to break; easy to keep to one page.
- **Cons:** Its student-oriented prompts need pruning for experienced applicants; the visual style is intentionally restrained; it has no ready-made executive summary.
- **Recommendation:** **Best overall.** Use this unless you have a specific reason to lead with a summary or a skills section.

### 2. University of Washington Downloadable Resume Template

**Download:** [Direct DOCX](https://cdn.uconnectlabs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/25/2026/01/Downloadable-Resume-Template-Microsoft-Word.docx) · [official page](https://careers.uw.edu/resources/sample-resumes/)

- **Layout:** One column with name/contact information, an optional Professional Summary, Education, Work Experience, Leadership Activities, and Additional Sections to Consider.
- **ATS compatibility:** Strong structural fit. The downloaded file has no body tables and no header/footer parts. Its conventional labels and bullet-first work history are easy to parse.
- **Ideal use case:** Students, early-career applicants, internship candidates, and career changers who need a concise summary to explain their target role.
- **Customization:** Replace the generic summary with a targeted two- or three-line summary, remove GPA and Dean's List if they are no longer relevant, and turn Additional Sections into Skills, Certifications, Projects, Publications, or Languages.
- **File formats:** Direct `.docx`.
- **Pros:** Flexible without becoming complex; explicitly prompts you to use job-description terms and measurable results; provides good room for projects and leadership.
- **Cons:** The default language is student-focused; the summary can become filler if it repeats the work history; technical skills are not given a prominent dedicated section by default.
- **Recommendation:** **Best flexible general template.** Pick it when a targeted summary will help make your transition or goal clear.

### 3. MIT Template A

**Download:** [Direct DOCX](https://cdn.uconnectlabs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/123/2022/01/Template-A.docx) · [official page](https://capd.mit.edu/blog/2023/09/01/enhance-your-resume-a-guide-for-first-year-undergraduates/)

- **Layout:** Education; Experience; Activities & Extracurriculars; Awards & Accomplishments; Skills & Interests. Education leads, and the rest follows a simple reverse-chronological pattern.
- **ATS compatibility:** Strong structural fit after personalization. The file has no body tables and no header/footer parts. MIT's broader guidance cautions against templates in general, which is why this should stay plain and should be rebuilt from the layout if you make major changes.
- **Ideal use case:** Current students and recent graduates whose coursework, awards, activities, and early projects are still relevant evidence.
- **Customization:** Replace MIT-specific placeholders, remove high school after it has lost relevance, move Experience above Education after you gain a sustained work history, and rename Activities to Projects or Leadership when that better matches your background.
- **File formats:** Direct `.docx`.
- **Pros:** Extremely simple reading order; gives a natural home to student activities and awards; no decorative elements to fight.
- **Cons:** No summary; less useful for an experienced applicant; the section order will look junior if you do not adapt it.
- **Recommendation:** **Best student and recent-graduate chronology.** Use it if academic context and extracurricular evidence should appear early.

### 4. MIT Template B

**Download:** [Direct DOCX](https://cdn.uconnectlabs.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/123/2022/01/Template-B.docx) · [official page](https://capd.mit.edu/blog/2023/09/01/enhance-your-resume-a-guide-for-first-year-undergraduates/)

- **Layout:** Education; Skills & Proficiencies; Professional Experience; Leadership Experience; Awards; Interests. It is still chronological, but it brings verifiable technical or procedural skills nearer the top.
- **ATS compatibility:** Strong structural fit after personalization. The file has no body tables and no header/footer parts. Keep the skill categories concrete and support them with achievements in Professional Experience.
- **Ideal use case:** Technical, engineering, data, lab, IT, or operations applicants whose tools, languages, platforms, or methods should be visible quickly.
- **Customization:** Group skills by type, such as Languages, Data, Cloud, Lab Methods, or Design Tools; move Skills after Experience if you have a long track record; replace Leadership Experience with Projects or Research if that is more relevant.
- **File formats:** Direct `.docx`.
- **Pros:** Makes legitimate hard skills easy to find; preserves a normal employment timeline; avoids the graphic skill bars that often cause parsing trouble.
- **Cons:** A long unprioritized skills list can read like keyword stuffing; education-first remains student-oriented; it needs evidence-rich bullets to avoid being merely a list of tools.
- **Recommendation:** **Best skills-forward template.** Use it for technical work, but keep the Work Experience section chronological and specific.

### 5. Jobscan Chronological Resume Template

**Download:** [Direct DOCX](https://static.jobscan.co/blog/uploads/MidLevel_Resume_Template_5.1-1.docx) · [official template page](https://www.jobscan.co/resume-templates-)

- **Layout:** A headline and Top Skills area, followed by Work Experience in reverse chronology and Education. The downloadable file begins with a promotional page; the usable resume begins after it.
- **ATS compatibility:** Good after cleanup. The main resume body has no Word tables, but you must delete the promotional page, instructional copy, and any unneeded branding before sending it. Keep the name, contact details, and work history in the main document body.
- **Ideal use case:** Mid-career applicants in formal or conventional fields who have a clear employment progression and want employers to see it first.
- **Customization:** Use a factual headline, tailor Top Skills to the job description, condense old or unrelated jobs, and preserve dates for every role. Delete all instructional paragraphs before exporting.
- **File formats:** Direct `.docx`.
- **Pros:** Strong chronological emphasis; room for skills without creating a separate functional resume; built by an ATS-focused company.
- **Cons:** Requires more cleanup than the university templates; contains a full promotional first page; its text prompts can make a resume verbose if left in place.
- **Recommendation:** **Best classic mid-career option.** Choose it when a steady work history is your strongest proof.

### 6. Jobscan Hybrid Resume Template

**Download:** [Direct DOCX](https://static.jobscan.co/blog/uploads/New_Hybrid_Resume-2-1.docx) · [official template page](https://www.jobscan.co/resume-templates-)

- **Layout:** Name/contact information, targeted summary, Skills and Accomplishments, chronological Work Experience, and Education. It combines a skills-forward opening with dated work history.
- **ATS compatibility:** Good after cleanup. The downloaded document has no body tables, but remove the promotional first page and all template instructions. Do not let the skills section replace a readable chronological experience section.
- **Ideal use case:** Career changers and mid-career applicants who need to connect transferable skills, measured accomplishments, and a visible employment timeline.
- **Customization:** Limit the summary to the role you want and the evidence you have; organize skills into three or fewer job-relevant categories; quantify accomplishments; keep dates, employers, titles, and locations consistent.
- **File formats:** Direct `.docx`.
- **Pros:** Lets you establish relevance before a recruiter reaches older jobs; balances skills with chronology; supports a targeted narrative.
- **Cons:** Easy to overfill; can resemble a functional resume if the work section becomes too thin; needs rigorous deletion of marketing and instructional text.
- **Recommendation:** **Best career-change template.** Use it only when the chronological Work Experience section remains easy to scan.

### 7. Jobscan Executive Resume Template

**Download:** [Direct DOCX](https://static.jobscan.co/blog/uploads/New_Executive_Resume_2-1-1.docx) · [official template page](https://www.jobscan.co/resume-templates-)

- **Layout:** Executive headline, contact information, reverse-chronological Professional Experience, and Education. The work-history prompts focus on scope, business impact, leadership, and measurable outcomes.
- **ATS compatibility:** Good after cleanup. Like the other Jobscan downloads, its resume body has no tables, but it ships with a promotional first page and guidance that must be deleted. Avoid adding charts, logos, or graphical scorecards when customizing.
- **Ideal use case:** Directors, vice presidents, executives, senior operators, and founders with roughly 10+ years of relevant leadership experience.
- **Customization:** Build a precise headline around role, sector, scale, and outcome; lead each role with business results; add Board Experience, Selected Transactions, or Certifications only if they improve the target application; trim early-career detail.
- **File formats:** Direct `.docx`.
- **Pros:** Correctly prioritizes business outcomes over task lists; works across two pages when justified; leaves room for leadership scope and financial impact.
- **Cons:** Too senior for most new graduates or individual contributors; the source download needs cleanup; a vague leadership headline will weaken the template.
- **Recommendation:** **Best executive option.** Use it if your candidacy is defined by leadership scale and quantified results.

## Before you submit: five ATS safety checks

1. **Use ordinary headings.** Prefer `Professional Experience`, `Education`, `Skills`, `Projects`, and `Certifications` over clever labels.
2. **Keep the reading order linear.** Put name, contact information, employment entries, locations, and dates in normal body text. Do not place essential content in headers, footers, text boxes, graphics, or sidebars.
3. **Avoid decorative structure.** Do not add logos, photos, skill-rating graphics, tables, or columns. MIT specifically lists tables, text boxes, colors, images, and graphics among formats to avoid for ATS compatibility. [MIT guidance](https://capd.mit.edu/blog/2023/09/01/enhance-your-resume-a-guide-for-first-year-undergraduates/)
4. **Tailor facts, not buzzwords.** Match exact terms from the job description only where they truthfully describe your experience. Harvard recommends specific, active, fact-based language and quantifiable results. [Harvard guidance](https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/resources/create-a-strong-resume/)
5. **Run a final text-order test.** Save the required format requested by the employer, then copy all text from the finished file into a plain-text editor. If the result does not read in a sensible top-to-bottom order, fix the document before uploading. Also inspect the application preview when the employer provides one.

## Recommendation in one sentence

Use the Harvard Bullet Point template for most applications; move to the University of Washington, MIT B, Jobscan Hybrid, or Jobscan Executive only when their specific layout better fits your career stage and the story your resume needs to tell.
